# 1903 Louisiana Historical Society exhibit: original catalogue page checked

**Source:** Louisiana Historical Society, *A Catalogue of the Colonial Exhibit of the Louisiana Historical Society and Loan Exhibit of Members and Friends* (New Orleans, 20 December 1903), **printed p. 17**, **PDF p. 19 (zero-indexed image 18)**, [public scan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/54/A_catalogue_of_the_colonial_exhibit_of_the_Louisiana_historical_society_and_loan_exhibit_of_members_and_friends_%28IA_catalogueofcolon00loui%29.pdf).

The original catalogue page was **visually inspected on 23 October 2026**. It lists:

- **Item 342**: the royal council retrocession decree, **23 January 1731**, listed as an exhibit loan by **Mr. Cusachs**.
- **Item 345**: the council decree concerning payment of Company of the Indies debts in Louisiana, **24 February 1731**, also listed as an exhibit loan by **Mr. Cusachs**.

**Correction to the earlier stub:** the items are on **printed page 17**, not printed page 18. The scan's PDF page index is 18 (page 19 in ordinary counting). The 1903 catalogue reports possession/loan at that time; it does **not** establish where either original printed copy is held today. Nor does a catalogue title prove legal debtor, amount paid, payment receipt or later settlement. The catalogue does not establish whether its copies correspond to the separate BnF 1731 or 1733 editions.
