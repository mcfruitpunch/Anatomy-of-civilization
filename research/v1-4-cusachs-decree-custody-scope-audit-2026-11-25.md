# V1.4 — Cusachs/Louisiana Historical Society collection-scope audit

**Research date:** 25 November 2026  
**Target:** doc-024, the Company of the Indies Louisiana debt decree dated 24 February 1731.  
**Priority:** Issue #24; the operative pages remain unread.

## 1. Why this retrieval task

The 1903 Louisiana Historical Society colonial loan-exhibit catalogue, printed p.17, item 345, lists the decree and credits it to “Mr. Gaspar Cusachs” in the Owner column. The 1904 Exposition Française catalogue, printed p.21, item 72, independently lists the title and date but no item-level owner. Those earlier catalogue examinations do not establish current custody.

I examined the Louisiana State Museum Historical Center's public collection inventories to find a more precise repository route for the Society's exhibit documentation and to cross-check, without conflating, later Cusachs collections.

## 2. RG23 — Louisiana Historical Society papers: priority retrieval route

The LSM collections inventory identifies **Record Group 23, Louisiana Historical Society (Papers of Robert Glenk)**, accession **T025.2003.13**, dates **6 December 1877–c.1939**, extent **6 linear feet**. The collection includes correspondence (1877–1939), minutes (1897–1919), announcements, invitations, membership lists and committee reports. The finding aid says most is arranged and additional items have since been identified and placed in the collection.

This is a concrete route for requesting the Society's 1903 colonial-exhibit register, loan paperwork, correspondence/minutes and any post-exhibit disposition record for items 342 and 345. **It is not evidence that the 1731 printed decree itself is held in RG23.** The online collection inventory does not itemize the decree or the relevant loan record.

Source: [LSM Historical Center manuscript collection inventory](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/58092328579fb31efea7a49e/t/5ecec9575167fa039ba9da3f/1590610266133/LHC_collectionsb.pdf), RG23, printed p.10, examined 25 Nov 2026.

## 3. RG431 — Gaspar Cusachs Loan: collection-level cross-check only

The same inventory lists **RG431, Gaspar Cusachs Loan**, accession range **00108–7675**, dates **1740–1925**, extent **1.5 linear feet and one oversize box**. The summary describes varied Louisiana materials, including land-grant requests, divorce records, David Morgan/Battle of New Orleans items, a Louisiana Bible Society ledger, emancipation/purchase/mortgage documents, plantation records, Civil War materials and later ephemera.

The summary does **not** itemize the 24 February 1731 decree or link RG431 to the 1903 catalogue's item 345. This is a possible provenance inquiry, not a current-custody match. The date range and summary are not a complete item-level inventory.

## 4. RG112 — Gaspar Cusachs Estate Collection: separate collection, important date-range caution

The April 2025 finding aid identifies **RG112, Gaspar Cusachs Estate Collection**, dates **1770–1904**, **2 boxes / 100 folders**, accession **09894.2–.123 / 2017.001.06–.12.5**. It is open for research, and digitized items are available on request. The majority was a gift of the Cusachs Estate in 1930; the remainder was purchased at auction in 2017.

The oversize list includes an **indenture dated 1763**, accession **09894.117**, outside the stated 1770–1904 collection date range. This is a useful caution: the date range is not a strict lower bound, so a date mismatch alone cannot prove the 1731 decree absent. The finding aid nevertheless does not itemize the debt decree or connect it to the 1903 loan-exhibit copy.

Source: [Gaspar Cusachs Estate Collection RG112 finding aid](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/58092328579fb31efea7a49e/t/67ffceca163f713a8669dad2/1744817866371/RG_112.pdff), descriptive summary, access/acquisition sections and oversize entry 09894.117, examined 25 Nov 2026.

## 5. Provenance assessment

**Documented catalogue facts**
- 1903 item 345 historically attributed to Mr. Gaspar Cusachs in the Owner column.
- 1904 item 72 lists the act/date, with no item-level owner.
- LSM RG23 provides a collection-level route to LHS correspondence/minutes from the 1903 exhibit period.
- RG431 and RG112 are separately described Cusachs loan/estate collections; neither finding aid identifies doc-024.

**Interpretation**
RG23 is the highest-priority request for records documenting the 1903 exhibit and item 345. RG431/RG112 are cross-checks only. No identity between the 1903 loan exhibit, the Cusachs loan/estate accessions, or any BnF/Columbia/LLMC print has been established.

**Unresolved**
- Whether the LHS register, exhibit loan correspondence or disposition file survives.
- Whether item 345 was retained, returned, sold, donated, or otherwise moved after the exhibit.
- Whether a specific Cusachs accession includes the decree.
- Current custody and physical-copy identity.
- Operative legal text, liability allocation, Crown assumption, collection, payment or settlement.

## 6. Affected-community and interpretive limits

These are collection and exhibit catalogues shaped by historical collecting and colonial administration. They do not represent Indigenous consent to French territorial claims or enslaved people's perspectives. The atlas retains Choctaw- and Peoria-authored context and records about manumission petitions separately; no human identity, transaction or consent is inferred from this provenance audit.
