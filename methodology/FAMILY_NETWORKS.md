# Family and intergenerational power records

Family research will use the same stable entity IDs and referenced edge records as the rest of the atlas; no ancestry-as-guilt logic is allowed.

## Relationship types

- `parent_of`, `sibling_of`, `married_to`: family/genealogical relationships.
- `led`, `directed`, `appointed`, `employed_by`: documented institutional positions.
- `owned`, `invested_in`, `financed`: financially evidenced connections.
- `communicated_with`, `coordinated_with`: independently documented communication or action.
- `interests_aligned_with`: interpretation, **not** a documented coordination edge unless substantiated separately.

Each edge should include dates when supportable, confidence/epistemic status, `verification`, sources and a narrative note describing exactly what the evidence establishes. Include links to counterevidence where available.

## First example: Dulles brothers, Iran 1953

The US State Department identifies John Foster Dulles and Allen W. Dulles as brothers serving as Secretary of State and CIA director. A July 28, 1953 telephone memorandum documents a conversation between them about wording for a statement concerning Iran. These are three separately recorded facts: kinship, two institutional offices, and a particular communication. The July 28 memorandum **does not by itself prove** what either brother intended about the coup or that the meeting planned the overthrow.

See `src-state-dulles-bio`, `src-cia-allen-bio`, `src-frus-dulles-1953`, and relationships `rel-014` to `rel-017`.

## Privacy and ethics

Focus on historically consequential public figures and well-documented public records. Do not map private addresses, private contact details, or uninvolved living relatives. Avoid treating ethnic, religious or national background as an indicator of intent. Scope expansion requires clear public relevance and review.
