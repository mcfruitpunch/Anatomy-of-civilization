          12 · INTEGRITY

          Research principles

          Evidence before explanation. Every documented event and relationship links to an identifiable source with a locator. Unresolved questions stay unresolved.

          Relationships are not allegations. Ancestry, belief, shared memberships or attendance at a meeting do not prove intent, coordination or culpability.

          History is revisable. We invite corrections, independent scholarship and historical perspectives underrepresented in official archives.
